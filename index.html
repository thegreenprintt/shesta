Questions

Common questions

What happens on a discovery call?

We talk through what you are making and what it is for. You will leave knowing the scope and the cost, whether or not you book.

How far in advance should I book?

Two to four weeks is comfortable for most sessions. Bigger brand or web projects need more runway. Rush dates are sometimes possible, so just ask.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. Sessions are based in Houston, with travel available worldwide for brand and editorial work.

Can photography and web design be combined?

Often the better buy. Shooting for a specific site means the photos and the layout are made for each other instead of forced together after the fact.

Is the day-trading education separate?

Yes. Trading education sits on 1House.TV as its own membership and has nothing to do with creative bookings. It is education only. Not financial advice, and not a promise of results.
