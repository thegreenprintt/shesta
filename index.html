06 — Questions

Good to know

What happens on a discovery call?

We talk through what you're creating, what it's for, and what success looks like. You'll leave with a clear sense of scope and cost — whether or not you book.

How far in advance should I book?

Two to four weeks is comfortable for most sessions. Larger brand or web projects benefit from more lead time, and rush availability can sometimes be arranged.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. Sessions are based in the Spring and greater Houston area, with travel available worldwide for brand and editorial work.

Can photography and web design be combined?

That's often the best value. Shooting imagery specifically for a site means the visuals and the design are built for each other instead of being forced together later.

Is the day-trading education separate?

Yes. Trading education is hosted on 1House.TV as its own membership and is unrelated to creative bookings. It's education only — not financial advice, and not a promise of results.
